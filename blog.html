Domestic 07 Aug 2024

Things to know when choosing a cleaning service

Choosing a cleaning team is about more than finding a convenient time. The right service should understand your priorities, communicate clearly and leave you confident about the care of your space.

Read the story

Start with your needs

Make a short list of the rooms or tasks that matter most. Think about whether you need a regular visit, a one-off deep clean, help before a move, or a specialist service. Sharing these details makes it easier to agree on a suitable scope.

Ask about the visit

Before booking, confirm what is included, what equipment and products are provided, how access is arranged and how changes to an appointment are handled. Clear expectations help the visit run smoothly.

Finally, choose a provider that welcomes questions and explains its approach. A good conversation is a helpful first sign that the service will be a good fit for your home.

Carpet Care 08 Aug 2024

A step-by-step guide to caring for your carpets

Carpets see everyday foot traffic, dust and the occasional spill. A simple routine helps them stay comfortable and presentable between professional cleans.

Read the story

Keep up with the basics

Vacuum regularly, taking a little extra time in entrances and other busy areas. Use a doormat and follow any care instructions supplied with your carpet, especially before trying a new cleaning product.

Act carefully on spills

Blot a fresh spill gently with a clean, absorbent cloth rather than rubbing it into the fibres. Test any suitable carpet cleaner on a hidden area first, and avoid soaking the carpet or mixing cleaning products.

When dirt is embedded or you need a more thorough refresh, professional carpet cleaning can help restore the feel of your floors. The right method depends on the carpet material and its care guidance.

Cleaning Tips 09 Aug 2024

How to build a simple, effective cleaning routine

A manageable routine can make cleaning feel less like a major project. Rather than trying to do everything at once, spread small tasks across the week and focus on the areas you use most.

Read the story

Make a short, repeatable plan

Start with everyday resets such as putting items away and wiping kitchen surfaces. Add a few weekly jobs—like vacuuming, bathroom care and changing towels—to a schedule that works for your household.

Keep supplies organised

Store appropriate cleaning supplies together and follow their labels. Never mix products, and use ventilation as directed. Adjust your routine seasonally or when your household’s needs change.

If your schedule is already full, a regular professional clean can take some recurring jobs off your list. You can choose a visit frequency and scope that suits your space.
